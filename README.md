Telehealth and Virtual Queue Management System

MediQueue SA is a web-based healthcare management prototype designed to improve patient access 
and support the management of queues in healthcare facilities. The system combines patient registration,
Ai-assisted symptom triage, virtual queue management, consultation records and administrative 
monitoring with one platform. 

The project aims to support a more organised patient journey by helping patients understand their urgency
classification and queue status, while providing healthcare professional with tools to manage consultations
and patient information. 


1. Project Objectives
   Mediqueue SA was developed to address challenges associated with:
   - Long waiting times and overcrowding in healthcare facilities
   - Manual patient prioritisation.
   - Limited visibility of patient queue positions.
   - Fragmented patient and consultation records
   - Administrative difficulties in monitoring patient flow and healthcare workloads.
The system uses digital workflows and AI-assisted services to support patient prioritisation and
queue management. It is designed to assist healthcare professionals, not replace their clinical judgement.

2. Main Features
   Patient Portal
   - Patient registration and login
   - Patient profile management
   - Symptom submission for AI-assisted urgency classification.
   - Urgency classification into Low, Moderate or High.
   - VIrtual Queue participation and queue-status monitoring.
   - Access to consultation information and relevant clinical documents
   - Hospital and referral-related functionality.

  Doctor Portal
  - Secure staff login.
  - Access to the prioritesed patient queue.
  - Review of relevant patient information.
  - Patient consultatio  management.
  - Recording of consultation notes and outcomes.
  - Prescription and referral functionality.

  Administrator Functionality
  - User and system management.
  - Monitoring of queue activity.
  - Consultation and operational reporting.
  - Visibility of doctor workload and system activity.
